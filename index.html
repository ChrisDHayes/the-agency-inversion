The Agency Inversion

What happens when the bank still holds your money—but an AI agent increasingly influences what happens to it?

Follow the shift from institution-mediated finance to agent-mediated decisions. Explore how the same capabilities can create extraordinary value and new forms of exposure—and why intelligence must be separated from authority.

01 / The structural shift

The institution holds the assets.
The agent may own the moment.

Traditional financial journeys revolve around institution-controlled channels. Agent-mediated finance can reorganize discovery, comparison and action across many providers—without removing the institution's regulated responsibilities.

Before: institution-mediated financeWhat changes in the emerging model?

The institution may retain custody, execution and regulated accountability while an AI agent increasingly shapes discovery, comparison, recommendation and customer intent.

  • Influence can move outside the institution before authority does.
  • Cross-provider comparison can increase customer value and switching pressure.
  • Delegation must be explicit, scoped, revocable and independently enforced.
  • Institutions still need evidence, reconciliation, recovery and accountable human judgement.

05 / The collective effect · V07

What if individually rational agents respond together?

An account recommendation may be sensible on its own. At scale, similar models reacting to similar signals could produce concentrated movement and institutional funding pressures. Explore a deliberately simplified closed-system scenario.

Interpretation: Adjust the controls and run the scenario.
Each institution starts with 25% of a normalized 100-unit system. Transfers only reallocate deposits among four invented banks. The model excludes external money, real balance sheets, liquidity reserves, payment timing, credit risk, depositor heterogeneity and regulatory intervention. It illustrates sensitivity to assumptions—not actual bank-run likelihood. Project Agenti studies wholesale-payment dynamics; this retail-deposit toy model is not its model or findings.

The Agency Inversion / Closing thesis

The next competitive advantage may not be controlling the interface.

It may be becoming the institution that customers—and their agents—trust to transform financial intentions into authorized, accountable and useful outcomes.

The model can create the recommendation. The customer defines the mandate. The institution must remain capable of enforcing the boundary.
